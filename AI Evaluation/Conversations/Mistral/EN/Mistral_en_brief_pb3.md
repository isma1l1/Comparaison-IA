## IA
In a Zener diode setup with a series resistance \( R \) and a DC input voltage \( V_{in} \), the intensity \( I \) of the current through the resistance \( R \) is given by:

\[
I = \frac{V_{in} - V_Z}{R}
\]

**Reasoning:**
- The Zener diode maintains a constant voltage \( V_Z \) across its terminals when it is in the breakdown region (reverse-biased).
- The voltage drop across the resistance \( R \) is \( V_{in} - V_Z \).
- By Ohm's Law, the current \( I \) through the resistance \( R \) is the voltage drop across \( R \) divided by \( R \).
