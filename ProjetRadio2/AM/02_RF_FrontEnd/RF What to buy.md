
---

## 🧾 1. Transistor (Q1)

### What to buy:
- **Type:** NPN small-signal transistor  
- **Examples (any one of these):**
  - BC547  
  - 2N2222  
  - 2N3904  

### Specs:
- Frequency capability: **> 1 MHz** (all of the above are fine)  
- Package: TO-92 (easy to use on breadboard)

👉 Recommendation:  
- Buy a **pack of BC547 or 2N3904** (cheap and versatile)

---

## 🔌 2. Resistors

You need standard resistors (¼ watt is fine).

### Values:
- **Rc:** 4.7 kΩ  
- **Re:** 1 kΩ  
- **Rb:** 100 kΩ  

### What to buy:
- Either:
  - Individual resistors  
  - OR a **resistor kit (recommended)**

👉 Recommendation:
- Buy a **resistor kit (E12 series)** → you’ll reuse it for the whole project

---

## ⚡ 3. Capacitors

### C_in (input coupling)
- Value: **10 nF – 100 nF**  
- Type: ceramic capacitor  

### C_out (output coupling)
- Value: **10 nF – 100 nF**  
- Type: ceramic capacitor  

### What to buy:
- Ceramic capacitor kit (values like 10 nF, 47 nF, 100 nF)

👉 Recommendation:
- Buy a **ceramic capacitor assortment kit**

---

## 🔋 4. Power Supply

### Options:
- **9V battery + clip (best for low noise)**  
- OR 5V regulated supply  

👉 Recommendation:
- Start with a **9V battery + battery clip**

---

## 🧱 5. Prototyping Materials

### Required:
- Breadboard (for testing)  
- Jumper wires  

### Optional:
- Perfboard (for final version)  
- Soldering kit  

---

## 🧰 6. Recommended Kits (Simplest Approach)

Instead of buying everything separately:

### 👉 Buy:
- 1× transistor pack (BC547 or 2N3904)  
- 1× resistor kit  
- 1× ceramic capacitor kit  
- 1× breadboard + jumper wires kit  

This will cover:
- RF Front-End  
- Detector  
- Audio amplifier
